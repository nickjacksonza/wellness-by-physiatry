What guides our care

Our Chicago-Based Mission & Values

Wellness by Physiatry exists to deliver culturally competent, multilingual care to Chicago's brain injury and neurological rehabilitation community. Here is what that mission means for your care, and where we are working to take it next.

Request an appointment (773) 312-4423
Our mission

Culturally competent, multilingual care that helps you heal, grow, and thrive

Wellness by Physiatry was created to deliver culturally competent, multilingual services designed to help you heal, grow, and thrive. That means care delivered in the language you are most comfortable in, and with an understanding of the culture, family and daily life you are coming back to, because those things shape how you heal as much as any care plan does.

Beyond your own care, our vision is to build a resourceful community: a place where survivors, families and caregivers can find answers to the day-to-day questions that come with brain injury, alongside the evidence-based rehabilitation medicine and lifestyle modifications that help you navigate your healthcare with confidence.

English Español Português

Culturally competent care

Your culture, your family and the life you are returning to shape how you heal, not just your diagnosis. We built Wellness by Physiatry to meet you where you are, in the language you are most comfortable in and with respect for the life circumstances you bring with you.

Whole-person, connected care

Your symptoms do not exist in isolation, and your care should not either. We look at how thinking, movement, mood and daily habits work together, so nothing about your recovery is treated as separate from anything else.

A resourceful, long-term community

Healing does not stop when your last office visit ends. We are working to build a resourceful community, so you, your family and your caregivers have answers to the questions that come after a brain injury, long after your care plan begins.
