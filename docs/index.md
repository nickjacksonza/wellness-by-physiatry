## Conditions we treat: TBI, stroke, dementia, encephalopathy, DAI, and anoxia

The umbrella in our logo means that we are all connected, and so are your symptoms.

Traumatic Brain Injury

A sudden impact to the head that causes damage to the brain.

Stroke

A lack of blood flow to certain parts of the brain.

Concussion

Health problems after brain injury like headaches, blurred vision, or trouble concentrating.

Cognitive Changes

An inability to think, learn, remember, or make decisions.

Neurobehavioral Changes

Changes in feelings and moods after a brain injury.

We also treat dementia, encephalopathy, diffuse axonal injury (DAI), a velocity-dependent injury that follows high falls, car accidents and falls from horses, and anoxia, a lack of oxygen to the brain.

## The three questions we hear most

### What conditions do you treat?

We care for survivors of any type of brain injury or acquired brain condition: traumatic brain injury (TBI) of all severities, including concussion, along with stroke, dementia, encephalopathy, diffuse axonal injury, and anoxic brain injury. These conditions can affect thinking, behavior, and function, and your family is part of your care.

### What makes your clinic different?

We combine evidence-based rehabilitation with lifestyle medicine and therapeutic coaching tailored to each patient. Our care is multilingual (English, Español, and Português), culturally competent, and rooted in whole-person healing. We also have the support of the University of Illinois Chicago Psychiatry Department, if we think your symptoms need more specialized attention.

### What insurance do you accept?

We accept most insurances, and because contracts are always changing, give us a call to confirm we take yours. We currently accept BCBS, Aetna, United, Medicare, Medicaid, and most marketplace plans. If you do not have [insurance](https://wellnessbyphysiatry.com/insurance-billing.html), we also offer a membership option.
