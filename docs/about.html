Dedicated to Your Recovery Journey and Long-Lasting Results

Wellness by Physiatry is a Chicago clinic built on a simple idea: your symptoms are connected, and your care should be too. Our story

Toward autonomy and care that makes sense to you

Education is key. At Wellness by Physiatry we believe it is important that you understand the changes you are experiencing in your brain and body. We help you move toward true autonomy by giving you the tools, and coaching you every step of the way, with the clarity you need to understand your own recovery. Instead of rushing through symptoms or offering fragmented advice, we slow down and teach you how your brain, body, and daily habits work together.

As a physiatrist specialized in brain injury, I spend time on the things that are important for you, connecting function, safety, and community. I pay close attention to how acquired brain conditions affect thinking, movement, swallowing, behavior, and overall health. I also integrate the six pillars of lifestyle medicine to help you make choices that support long-lasting results. The pillars refer to basic functions such as sleep, nutrition, keeping stress levels in check, regular exercise, and minimizing the use of risky substances such as recreational drugs and alcoholic beverages.

By blending evidence-based rehabilitation and practical lifestyle medicine guidance, we deliver compassionate and personalized care. Wellness by Physiatry creates a patient experience that feels empowering, transparent, and genuinely supportive. It's care that helps people not just get better, but understand how to stay better.

The umbrella in our logo means that we are all connected, and so are your symptoms.

Dr. Fabiolla Kopp, MD Founder and physiatrist

Education comes first

Good decisions about your recovery start with understanding what is happening to you. After a traumatic brain injury, stroke, or concussion, Dr. Kopp explains the changes in your thinking, movement, mood, and energy in plain language, as many times as it takes, and teaches you how your brain, body, and daily habits work together. That understanding turns a care plan from something done to you into something you can carry forward on your own, with your family and caregivers beside you.

Care that connects the whole picture

Dr. Kopp is a physiatrist who specializes in brain injury rehabilitation medicine, so she looks at function, safety, and community together. She pays close attention to how acquired brain conditions, including traumatic brain injury, stroke, diffuse axonal injury, and anoxic brain injury, affect thinking, movement, swallowing, behavior, and overall health status. You see the same physician at every visit, at our East Loop or Lincoln Park location in Chicago, and she coordinates the rest of your care so it stays connected.

Rebuilding strength, restoring function

We combine evidence-based rehabilitation care plans with practical lifestyle medicine guidance, including health coaching and group sessions in English, Español, and Português. When hands-on therapy is the right next step, such as physical, occupational, or speech therapy, we refer you to trusted partners and stay involved in your care. The result is compassionate, personalized care that helps you get better and understand how to stay better.

Lifestyle medicine

The pillars we build habits on

The pillars refer to basic functions that shape how well you recover.

  • Sleep restorative rest, treated as part of your care plan rather than an afterthought.
  • Nutrition what you eat, adjusted to your own overall health status and goals.
  • Stress keeping stress levels in check with practical, repeatable habits.
  • Exercise regular movement, paced to where you are in your recovery.
  • Risky substances minimizing the use of substances that work against your recovery, talked through without judgment.
  • Social connection staying connected to people who understand what you are going through.
