The three questions we hear most

What conditions do you treat?

We specialize in recovery from all severities of traumatic brain injury (TBI) including concussion. We also serve those with residual deficits from stroke and other neurologic conditions that affect thinking, behavior, and function.

What makes your clinic different?

We combine evidence-based rehabilitation with lifestyle medicine and cognitive rehabilitation tailored to each patient. Our care is multilingual (English, Español, and Português), culturally competent, and rooted in whole-person healing. We also have the support of the University of Illinois Chicago Psychiatry Department, if we think your symptoms need more specialized attention.

What insurance do you accept?

We accept most insurances and because contracts are always changing, give us a call to confirm if we take your insurance. We currently accept Aetna, BCBS, Cigna, United, Medicare, Medicaid, and some other plans. No insurance, no problem — we also offer affordable membership options.
