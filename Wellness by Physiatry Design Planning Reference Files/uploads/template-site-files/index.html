The three things everyone asks.

Do you deliver outside Johannesburg?

Yes. Joburg and Pretoria are next-day by our own driver. Everywhere else in South Africa goes by courier and lands in two to three working days.

Can I pause or cancel?

Any time, from the link at the bottom of every delivery email. No phone call, no retention script, no minimum term.

What if I do not like the coffee?

Tell us within a week and we will swap the bag or refund it. We would rather re-roast than have you drinking something you do not enjoy.

Still deciding?

Come and taste all three at the roastery on a Saturday morning. No booking needed.
