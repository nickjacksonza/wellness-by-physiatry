Before you commit.

Is there a minimum term?

No. Pause, change plan or cancel from the link in any delivery email. Changes made before Sunday apply to the next delivery.

Can I change my grind later?

Yes, and it is the single most common change people make. Reply to any delivery email and we will update it before the next roast.

What happens over December?

The roastery closes for two weeks. We email in November so you can pull your last delivery forward or pause until January.

Not sure which plan?

Most people who drink one to two cups a day land on Duo. If in doubt, start there and drop down later, it is a two-click change.
